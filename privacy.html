Review copy · 18 September 2026. This notice describes the implemented app and backend. Provider retention settings and transfer arrangements must be confirmed before publication.

Account and sign-in
Apple, Google or your existing email/password sign-in authenticates your account through Supabase. Supabase holds account identifiers, sign-in information and your email address where supplied. An Apple private relay email may be used. This information authenticates you, secures your session and links account-based features. Your protected role determines author and administrator access. Account & Support shows your linked sign-in methods, not necessarily which one you used most recently.

Study information
Study answers, saved mistakes and progress are stored on this device. They are not currently synced to your account. People using different Wingademy accounts on the same device share that device’s saved study progress. Signing out does not clear it; Reset all study progress in the Logbook clears the saved study progress. App data removal can also remove local records. Settings and accepted Terms version/date are stored locally.

Crew
Your display name, selected city, language, preparation stage, group membership, invitation requests, messages, blocks and reports are stored in Supabase. City is chosen manually; this build does not request GPS location. Group members can see shared profile and chat information. These records enable study groups, invitations, messaging, blocking and moderation. Administrators can review reports and moderate content. Chat is not end-to-end encrypted.

News briefings
Headlines, short summaries and optional source credits/links are stored in Supabase. Published briefings are visible to readers. Authors and administrators have role-based editing access. This version has no news photo upload or display. Previously uploaded News photos have been removed from the active News storage bucket; the bucket was confirmed empty during publication preparation. Avoid including personal or confidential information that should not be published.

Purchases
Apple handles purchase authentication and payment information. Wingademy does not receive your payment-card details. Supabase stores verified transaction identifiers, account bindings and purchase status. A verifier hosted on Render receives Apple's signed transaction, including the app account token, to check its signature. The service verifies production purchases and permits Sandbox verification only for designated test accounts. Verification is refreshed online; paid question access requires a verification less than one hour old. Apple refunds or revocations remove access when rechecked or when the cached verification expires. Apple offer-code redemption sends signed purchase proof to the verification server. A reservation prevents a redeemed purchase from being linked to multiple accounts. After account deletion, the reservation retains the Apple transaction identifier without the Wingademy user reference to prevent reassignment. Server notifications are not connected in this build.

Support email
If you choose Open email draft, your email application receives the draft addressed to 30millionbro@gmail.com. You review and send it yourself. Question reports include the question ID, chapter, revision, prompt and handbook reference along with the notes you enter. They do not automatically include your Wingademy account email, authentication tokens or purchase identifiers. Your email service normally reveals your sender address when you send. Support correspondence is handled in the operator's Gmail mailbox. Resolved support-email conversations are deleted manually from the support mailbox within 12 months after the issue is resolved. They may be kept longer where needed for an ongoing dispute or a legal requirement. This is an operator-managed process, not automatic deletion by the App; Gmail may retain separate copies under its own policies.

Service providers and links
Supabase provides authentication and database storage. An empty storage bucket remains from the former News photo feature. Render assists with purchase verification. Apple and Google provide the selected sign-in services; Gmail is used for support correspondence. These services may process data outside your country. These information pages are intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits without a GitHub login. External links open services with their own privacy practices. No advertising SDK or separate analytics SDK has been added in this build; infrastructure providers may keep operational and security logs.

Deletion and controls
Signing out is not account deletion. Use Logbook → Account & Support → Delete account to request permanent deletion. This removes your active account, profile, Crew membership, messages, reports linked to you, news contributions and associated stored photos, and account-linked purchase records. Ownership of a Crew transfers to a remaining member; empty Crews are deleted. Apple-linked accounts require Apple authorization revocation. Cleanup may take multiple attempts if interrupted; retry from the same page. Local study progress on this phone is cleared after confirmed success. Copies on other devices, previously downloaded content and routine backups may persist separately; contact the operator about those copies and retention. Deletion does not delete your Apple or Google account or issue a refund. The Apple offer reservation described above remains without the deleted Wingademy user reference. Apple may retain its own transaction and account-token records under its policies.

Retention — review required
The operator currently uses the Supabase Free plan and has not made manual database backups or user-data exports. This does not establish that service providers hold no operational, security or recovery copies.

Active account, Crew and purchase records remain in the backend until removed through the relevant app or account-deletion functions; these records do not currently have a general automatic expiry. Purchase verification expiring after one hour does not delete the record. Local study progress remains until reset, deleted with the account on that device, or removed with app data. The offer-claim reservation currently has no automatic expiry. Render’s Hobby workspace makes dashboard logs available for 7 days. Supabase Free makes API and database logs available for 1 day and does not include automatic database backups as a plan feature. These availability windows do not establish deletion of every provider-held operational, security or recovery copy. Provider processing arrangements remain under review before final publication.
