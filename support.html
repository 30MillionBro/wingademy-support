Wingademy is a study aid for aspiring pilots. It is not an operational flight reference or an approved training course.

Contact us

Email 30millionbro@gmail.com for app help, question corrections, account access, purchases, privacy requests or community concerns. Include your app version/build, a description of the issue and the exact error if available. Please do not include passwords, offer codes, private keys or full purchase receipts.

Full Access and offer codes

Sign in through Crew before purchasing or linking a redeemed Apple offer. Full Access belongs to the Wingademy account linked to that purchase. Open Cadet logbook → Full Access to restore or verify it. Use the same Apple purchase account and Wingademy account. An internet connection is required. If the app reports an account mismatch, contact support rather than buying again.

Redeem Apple offer codes through Apple’s redemption screen. If prompted, choose Link redeemed offer and confirm the correct Wingademy account. Each redeemed purchase links to one account.

For purchase refunds, use Apple’s refund request service. Eligibility is determined through Apple’s process and applicable consumer rights.

Study progress

Progress is saved on the device and is not synced between devices or separated by Wingademy login. Use the three-dot chapter menu to reset that chapter’s completion. Reset all study progress in the Logbook clears the local study record.

Account deletion

Open Cadet logbook → Account & Support → Delete account. Review the deletion details before confirming. Deletion removes the Wingademy account and its account-linked access; it does not delete your Apple/Google account or issue a refund. An existing purchase does not automatically transfer to a newly created Wingademy account. Contact support if you cannot sign in to request deletion.

Crew safety

Use the report and block options for inappropriate messages or users. You can also email support. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in chat.
